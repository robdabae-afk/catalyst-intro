# How to read a Form C

Form C is filed for a Reg CF offering and contains information required by Regulation Crowdfunding. The SEC’s issuer disclosure rule lists items such as the company’s business and officers, ownership and capital structure, use of proceeds, target and maximum offering amount, deadline, price or method for determining price, related-party transactions, financial statements, and risk factors. The exact filing and exhibits should be read together.

A practical sequence is to identify the security first. Is it stock, a SAFE, a convertible note, or another instrument? Then check the offering amount and deadline. The target is not always the same as the maximum. Review the use-of-proceeds table and ask what the company expects to do if it raises only part of the target. Next, read who owns the company and what securities could convert or be issued later. Compare financial statements with the company’s narrative, noting their date and whether they are certified, reviewed, or audited.

For example, a fictional company may say it is raising up to $600,000, with a $300,000 target. If it raises only the target, it may have a smaller operating budget than the pitch’s full plan suggests. The filing is not a seal of approval, and its availability on EDGAR does not mean the SEC endorses the investment. Search the filing history for amendments and read the actual security agreement before deciding.
