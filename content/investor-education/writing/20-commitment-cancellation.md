# Investment commitment and cancellation

An investment commitment is not the same as adding an item to a shopping cart. Before submitting, verify the issuer, security, amount, fees, offering deadline, and payment instructions. Read the final confirmation and save it. Reg CF has specific cancellation and reconfirmation rules; do not assume the same rules apply to every private security or that every platform uses identical button labels.

For a Reg CF offering, an investor generally may cancel a commitment for any reason until 48 hours before the offering deadline. Once the offering is within 48 hours of its end, the investor generally cannot cancel for any reason, even if the commitment was made during that final period. If the issuer makes a material change to the offering terms or other disclosed information, investors must receive five business days to reconfirm. If the investor does not reconfirm as required, the intermediary applies the rule’s process.

For example, if an offering closes Friday at 5 p.m., the general 48-hour point is Wednesday at 5 p.m. That is an illustration only; the platform’s official deadline and time zone control. A material-change notice can create a separate reconfirmation period. Read the intermediary’s messages promptly and follow its stated process. If you are unsure about the investment itself, do not rely on a cancellation window as a substitute for reviewing the terms beforehand. This lesson summarizes Reg CF concepts; consult the offering platform and qualified counsel for a specific commitment.
