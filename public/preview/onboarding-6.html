Before you invest

Three things worth knowing.

01

Most startups fail.

You could lose everything you put in. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

02

Your money is locked up.

Plan on years, not months. Payouts only come if the company sells or goes public.

03

It's hard to sell.

There's no stock market for these shares. You usually can't sell for at least a year.
