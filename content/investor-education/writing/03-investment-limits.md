# Reg CF investment limits

Neither formula is a per-company allowance. Whichever tier applies, a non-accredited investor’s total across all Reg CF offerings cannot exceed $124,000 in any rolling twelve-month period. The limit applies across portals, not just one company or app. The SEC’s investor bulletin states the individual calculation, and SEC Corporation Finance interpretations were updated July 9, 2026. Always check the current rule and your intermediary’s calculation.

Here are two fictional calculations. If annual income is $50,000 and net worth is $40,000, the greater figure is $50,000. Five percent is $2,500, so the greater of $2,500 or $2,500 is $2,500. If annual income is $150,000 and net worth is $80,000, the lower-threshold formula applies because one measure is below $124,000. Five percent of the greater figure, $150,000, is $7,500, so the 12-month limit is $7,500. If both income and net worth are at least $124,000, use ten percent of the greater figure, with an overall cap of $124,000.

The SEC describes special treatment for a primary residence in the net-worth calculation. In broad terms, the home’s value is excluded, and certain mortgage debt is excluded up to the home’s fair market value; recent increases in home-secured borrowing can count. Joint spousal calculations have specific rules too. Do not estimate casually from home equity or rely on one portal’s remaining balance as a complete record. Keep your own running total across platforms, and ask a tax or legal professional if your circumstances are unusual.
